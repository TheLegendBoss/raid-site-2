# UI assets

- `wns_*.png`: regions cut from **Waste No Space GUI** (`waste_no_space_64x64_gui.png`) by Flatus, CC0 1.0.
  Used as 9-slice panels, buttons, fields and tabs (2 px borders) by `src/ui/pixelUi.ts`.
- `icon_*.png`: from the **Mystery Attic icon set** (UI), free for commercial use, credit optional.
  `icon_blade.png` is our own, in the same style (`art/uiicons.py`).
- `xh_*.png`: crosshair arms and centre cut from **160+ Cursors Crosshairs (32x32)** by KabGames, CC0 1.0.
- `../fonts/Poco.ttf`: **Poco** by fontenddev (https://fontenddev.com/fonts/poco), licensed
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit is required; it is shown on the title screen.
