# Pixel weapon parts

`body/`, `barrel/`, `stock/`, `scope/` are a subset of **PIXWEP - modular weapons** by Zintoki,
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). They are composed and recoloured at runtime by
`src/fx/pixGun.ts`; kits live in `src/data/gunKits.ts`.

`barrel_{suppressor,brake,flared,twin}`, `stock_{buttplate,wire,cheek,ring}`, `scope_{iron,prism,long,reflex}`,
`under/`, and `mag/` are our own, generated by `art/pixparts/build.py`.
